ABOUT ME

Editing is more than software.
It's understanding people.

I'm a creative video editor driven by storytelling, emotion and strategy.

What fascinates me most about cinema and advertising is their ability to shape how people feel through every frame, every cut and every sound.

Before I start editing, I focus on understanding the client's brief, the audience and the purpose behind the project. My goal isn't to create what I personally like—it's to deliver a final result that reflects the client's vision while communicating clearly with the audience.

Every project is another opportunity to improve, experiment and create work that people remember.

Marketing Mindset
